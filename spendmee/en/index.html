FAQ

How do I turn on automatic Apple Pay capture?

After you sign in, the app walks you through a guide (you can also find it in Settings). In Shortcuts, you create a personal automation with the Transaction trigger that runs the “Record payment” action. Turn off “Notify When Run” for it. After your first payment, Settings will show “Last payment captured”.

A payment wasn’t recorded. What should I check?

In Shortcuts, make sure the automation is turned on and set to “Run Immediately”. Only Apple Pay payments made with a card in Wallet are captured. You can always add an expense manually.

Where is my data?

Expenses are synced to a database in the EU (Frankfurt). We don’t sell them, show ads or use third-party analytics. You’ll find the details in the privacy policy.

What’s in Premium?

Everything personal is free, with no limit on the number of expenses. Premium unlocks creating groups and adding entries to them. Premium Family is shared through Apple Family Sharing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SpendMee. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible; everything is physically removed from the server within 31 days. Before that, you can export your expenses to CSV.
