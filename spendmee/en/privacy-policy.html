Privacy Policy

SpendMee for iPhone

Česky

This policy describes how the SpendMee app for iPhone (the “app”) processes the personal data of its users under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll., on the Processing of Personal Data. In the event of any inconsistency between the Czech and English versions of this policy, the Czech version prevails.

1. Controller and contact

The controller of personal data is Vojtěch Volprecht (the “controller”, “we” or “us”).

Please send questions and requests regarding personal data to brainvault.mind@gmail.com. Include your user ID in the email (in the app under Settings → Privacy; tap it to copy it), so we can find you in our data. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as this is not mandatory given the scope of our processing.

2. What data we process

Account and profile

  • the identifier of your Apple ID from Sign in with Apple (the anonymous “sub”) and an internal user ID;
  • your display name (taken from Apple at first sign-in; you can change it at any time);
  • your default currency and the device time zone.

We do not store your email address. The app does not request it, even if Apple offers it.

Expenses and settings

  • expenses you enter manually, by voice, or that the app captures from Apple Pay via Shortcuts: amount, currency, merchant, category, date and time, card name from Wallet, source of the entry;
  • limits, category and card rules, muted notifications;
  • group memberships, invitations, and the expenses you add to a group.

Voice input is converted to text directly on your device (Apple on-device speech recognition); neither the audio nor the transcript is sent to us, only the resulting expense.

Subscription

  • the status of your Premium subscription (expiry date, product, whether it is shared via Family Sharing). We do not see your payment details; payment is processed by Apple.

Technical data

  • the device push token and environment (for limit notifications and group events);
  • technical server records (e.g. the IP address in a request log) kept by the hosting provider for its standard retention period.

What we do not process: we do not use third-party analytics or crash reporting tools, we do not track you across apps and websites (no App Tracking Transparency), we do not show ads, and we do not carry out profiling or automated decision-making. We do not send any data to cloud-based artificial intelligence; expense and category recognition runs on your device. We only see aggregated download and crash statistics in Apple’s App Store Connect, depending on your sharing settings in iOS.

3. Purposes and legal basis

We process all data in order to provide you with the app under the contract (the terms of use) you enter into with us by registering – legal basis: performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Specifically:

  • maintaining your account and Sign in with Apple;
  • storing, syncing and summarizing your expenses and converting currencies (Czech National Bank (ČNB) exchange rates);
  • limits and notifications when they are exceeded;
  • groups: sharing your name and the expenses you add to a group with the other members (the app notifies you of this when you accept an invitation);
  • the Premium subscription and verifying your entitlement to it;
  • support and handling your requests.

We do not ask for your consent, because the app cannot work without this data. You grant iOS permissions (notifications, microphone and speech recognition) in the system and can turn them off at any time.

5. How long we keep data

  • For as long as you have an account. We do not automatically delete inactive accounts.
  • A deleted expense is hidden immediately and physically deleted within 31 days (a cleanup runs daily and deletes records older than 30 days).
  • Deleting your account (in the app, Settings → Delete account) is immediate and irreversible for you: your expenses also disappear from groups, your profile and settings are removed, and your Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked. We physically delete everything on the server within 31 days; until then, a new account cannot be created with the same Apple ID. The hosting provider’s backups expire according to its standard cycle.
  • A closed group is read-only for 30 days, then ceases to exist and is physically deleted within a further 30 days.
  • The push notification queue on the server: at most 90 days.
  • Data on your device (the app’s local database) is deleted when you sign out or delete your account. In internal test builds of the app, a debug log of captured Apple Pay payments is additionally kept on the device (rotated after 30 days / 500 entries); it is turned off in the App Store version and is never sent to the server.

Deleting your account does not end your Premium subscription – cancel it in your Apple ID settings (the app will direct you there). Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase data in accordance with their own policies and accounting regulations.

6. Your rights

You have the right:

  • of access to your data (Art. 15) – write to the support email with your user ID;
  • to rectification (Art. 16) – change your name and currency in Settings, and your expenses directly in the app;
  • to erasure (Art. 17) – by deleting your account in the app, or via the support email;
  • to restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • to data portability (Art. 20) – export all your expenses to CSV in Settings → Data;
  • to object (Art. 21); however, processing based on the contract cannot be stopped without closing your account;
  • not to be subject to automated decision-making (Art. 22) – we do not carry out any.

We will respond to your request without undue delay, and within one month at the latest.

If you believe we handle your data in breach of the regulations, you can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, Czech Republic, www.uoou.gov.cz.

7. Security

Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, each user has access only to their own data and to the groups they are a member of (access control directly in the database). On the device, the local database is protected by iOS file encryption (accessible after the phone is first unlocked, so that a payment can be recorded even with the screen locked). Sign-in is exclusively via Apple; you have no password for the app.

8. Children

The app is not intended for persons under the age of 15.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, e.g. when adding a new feature or a new processor. We will inform you of material changes in the app. The current version is available in the app (Settings → Privacy Policy) and at this address.
